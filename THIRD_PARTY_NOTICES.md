## Casio CZ MIDI specification

The conversions between CZ front-panel values and the values the sound chip takes come from
Casio's CZ-101/1000/5000 MIDI specification, and the envelope conversions from Casio's tables as
kasploosh.com reproduced them ("Casio CZ Envelopes").

Casio and CZ are trademarks of Casio Computer Co., Ltd. Blackbox Drums is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Casio.
